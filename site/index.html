MatEnforcer

Software for Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu academies, built by a practitioner.

MatEnforcer runs a BJJ academy the way the gym already works: belts and stripes, mat hours, Gi and No-Gi, open mats and drop-ins. It's launching soon.

At launch

Everything below is planned for the first release. None of it is live yet.

On the mat

  • Check-in in one step. At the counter kiosk, by QR code or by name, with the class schedule, class booking with waitlists, and mat hours counted per Gi and No-Gi track. You set how far ahead members can book, unconfirmed spots can go to the waitlist, and no-shows are recorded.

  • A whole class checked in at once. Pick members from the class list, or mark everyone booked as present, and mat hours count for each of them.

  • A coach's screen for the mat. A round timer you can read across the room, and who is in class with their rank, first class, birthday and how close they are to promotion.

  • Promotion rules. You decide how much attendance counts. Stripe and belt eligibility can weigh mat hours, attendance, time in grade, syllabus check-offs and instructor assessments, on separate Gi and No-Gi ladders, with optional belt testing. Eligibility tells the instructor. It never promotes anyone by itself.

  • Achievements. Badges for attendance streaks, mat-hour milestones and class counts, and leaderboards each academy can turn on.

  • Curriculum and recorded classes. Technique libraries and recorded mat sessions that students watch in the member app.

  • Open mats, seminars and privates. Booked and paid for in the app, with tickets and fees.

Running the academy

  • A daily needs-attention list. The members and prospects to act on today, each with a plain reason, like no visit in 14 days, a failed payment, a trial with no follow-up or a student ready for promotion.

  • A member's tasks in one place. Change a plan, freeze a membership, update a family's card, record a rank and send a message from one profile. Or select a filtered group of members and message, freeze or tag them all at once.

  • A roster with belts and stripes. Every member with their rank, and roles for owner, admin, instructor, front desk and student, invited by email.

  • Online sign-up and waivers. New members sign on their phone or at the kiosk, and a parent or guardian signs for a child.

  • Documents on a member's profile. Upload signed paper forms, medical notes or ID, kept private and erased with the member.

  • Dues and drop-ins. Through your own payment processor, with freezes, pauses, family billing, class packs, trials, promo codes and renewal reminders. A frozen membership is never charged.

  • Failed payments recovered. The member gets a link to update their card, retries run on your schedule, staff hear when retries run out, and the member's status comes back once they pay.

  • Instructor pay. Rates per class, per hour or per head, and a report of each instructor's classes, hours and amount owed for the pay period.

  • Expenses. Record rent, stock and other costs, including repeating ones, and see monthly income, expenses and net.

  • Reports. Revenue, attendance and churn over time, each downloadable as CSV.

  • More than one location. One academy with several locations, each with its own schedule, kiosk and staff.

  • Move in, and move out. Import members, ranks and attendance from your current software's CSV exports, with a checklist that walks you through the switch, and export all your data any time.

  • Support inside the app. Ask for help from the portal and read the replies in the same thread.

Messages and growth

  • Messages that bring students back. An automatic email or text when someone has been away too long, reaches a milestone or misses a payment, plus broadcasts to the members you choose.

  • Automations you set. Pick a trigger, like days away, a trial attended or a waiver expiring, and an action: send a message, tell staff or give someone a task. Defaults come built in, and you can change or turn off any of them.

  • Class reminders. A reminder before a booked class, and a notice when a class is cancelled or moved, by email, text or push.

  • Texts both ways. Read and answer member and prospect texts from a shared inbox, with saved replies.

  • Email from your own domain. Broadcasts and reminders come from your academy's address once you verify the domain.

  • Prospects to members. Follow trial sign-ups and walk-ins from first contact to joining. Every prospect has an owner and a next step, and anyone left waiting gets flagged.

  • Referrals. Members share a personal link or QR code, and you set the reward when a friend joins.

Online

  • A free member app. It installs on a phone, with one passkey sign-in for every academy a member trains at.

  • A training journal. Members note techniques and rolls against each class, private unless they share them, and record their competition results.

  • Your academy's website. Schedule, staff profiles with lineage and your YouTube videos, on your own domain or a matenforcer.net address. Enter a closure or class change once and it shows on the site, in the app and at the kiosk.

  • A gear shop. Sell your academy's gear online, in sizes, with stock tracked.

  • Affiliation teams. Show your team on your site, and carry a member's rank to another gym on the same team.

  • Build your own site. A content API and webhooks for academies with their own developers.

  • Connect your own tools. Webhooks when a member joins, checks in, is promoted or misses a payment.
