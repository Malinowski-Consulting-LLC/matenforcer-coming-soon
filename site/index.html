At launch

Everything below is planned for the first release. None of it is live yet. Six things owners ask for first, then the rest of the first release, in short.

What matters most

  1. 01Belts and stripes, tied to attendance. See who is ready for the next belt or stripe, and why. Mat hours, time in grade and syllabus work all count.

  2. 02Failed payments recovered. A backup card and a member wallet stop most failures. The rest get a card update link and retries on your schedule.

  3. 03Quiet members flagged before they quit. A drop against a member's own usual attendance triggers a message, not just days away.

  4. 04One bill for the whole family. Kids, parents and siblings on one account, one card, one payment.

  5. 05Check-in, schedule and booking in one place. Kiosk, QR code or name at the door, with class booking, waitlists and mat hours counted per Gi and No-Gi track.

  6. 06Reports and bulk actions that save an evening. Revenue, attendance and churn over time, and message, freeze or tag a whole filtered group at once.

Everything else at launch

The full first release, in short.

On the mat

  • Check-in in one step. Kiosk, QR code or name, with booking and mat hours per track.
  • A whole class checked in at once. Mark everyone booked as present.
  • A coach's screen for the mat. Round timer, ranks and promotion progress in class.
  • Promotion rules. Eligibility from mat hours, time in grade, syllabus and instructor checks.
  • Achievements. Streak and mat-hour badges, with optional leaderboards.
  • Curriculum and recorded classes. Technique libraries and recorded sessions in the app.
  • Open mats, seminars and privates. Booked and paid in the app, with tickets and fees.
  • In-house tournaments. Brackets, mat schedules and scoring, results back to each record.

Running the academy

  • A daily needs-attention list. Who to act on today, with a plain reason.
  • A member's tasks in one place. Plan, freeze, card, rank and message from one profile.
  • A roster with belts and stripes. Ranks, roles and federation numbers and expiry dates.
  • Online sign-up and waivers. Sign on a phone or at the kiosk, guardian signs for a child.
  • Documents on a member's profile. Forms, medical notes and ID, kept private.
  • Dues and drop-ins. Your own processor, with freezes, family billing, class packs and trials.
  • Failed payments recovered. Backup card, member wallet, update links and scheduled retries.
  • Instructor pay. Rates per class, hour or head, with a pay period report.
  • Expenses. Rent, stock and repeating costs, with monthly income and net.
  • Reports. Revenue, attendance and churn over time, downloadable as CSV.
  • More than one location. One academy, several locations, members attend at any location.
  • Franchise support. One console for fees, royalties and payouts. $100 a month flat.
  • Door access tied to membership. Doors open for members in good standing only.
  • Move in, and move out. Import from CSV exports with a guided checklist, export any time.
  • Support inside the app. Ask for help and read replies in the same thread.

Messages and growth

  • Messages that bring students back. Automatic email or text on a pattern drop, milestone or missed payment.
  • Automations you set. A trigger and an action, with defaults built in.
  • Class reminders. Reminders before class, notices when a class moves, trial and review nudges.
  • Texts both ways. A shared inbox for member and prospect texts, with saved replies.
  • An AI front desk. Answers texts, calls and social messages, books trials, hands off to staff.
  • Post to social from one place. Schedule posts and answer DMs in one inbox.
  • Email from your own domain. Broadcasts and reminders from your academy address.
  • Prospects to members. Trials and walk-ins tracked from first contact to joining.
  • Referrals. Personal links and QR codes, with a reward you set.

Online

  • A free member app, customized for your academy. Installs on a phone, one passkey sign-in.
  • A training journal. Techniques, rolls and competition results, private unless shared.
  • Your academy's website. Schedule, staff and lineage, on your own domain, with an optional blog.
  • A gear shop. Gear online, in sizes, with stock tracked.
  • Affiliation teams. Show your team, carry a rank to another gym on the team.
  • Five languages at launch. English, Spanish, Portuguese, French and German, Japanese to follow.
  • Build your own site. A content API and webhooks for your own developers.
  • Connect your own tools. Webhooks when a member joins, checks in, is promoted or misses a payment.

Coming after launch

Planned for a later release. None of this is in the first release, and none of it is live yet.

  • At-home homework drills. Drills sent to a student's phone, scored on the device by the camera, with nothing recorded or uploaded.
  • In-app academy chat. Group chats per program, direct messages with instructors, polls and announcements in the member app.
  • Student loyalty points. Points for attendance and milestones, with rewards the academy defines.
  • Japanese. Japanese joins the five launch languages.
  • AI-assisted lesson planning. A draft class plan from the curriculum and the students attending, for the instructor to edit.
  • Smoothcomp integration. Tournament registrations and results from Smoothcomp, written back to each member's record.
